# FMCG Data-Analysis
## Project Objective
the objective of this FMCG data analysis project is to evaluate overall sales performance by analyzing product-wise, store-wise, and SKU-level data to identify key business trends. The project aims to highlight top-performing and fast-moving products, assess each store’s contribution to total revenue, and understand SKU contribution patterns. By transforming raw sales data into meaningful insights and visual dashboards, the analysis supports data-driven decision-making in areas such as inventory planning, pricing optimization, and sales strategy improvement within the FMCG domain.
## Dataset Used

## Questions(KPI's)
- Total Sales Revenue – Measures the overall revenue generated from FMCG product sales.
- Total Quantity Sold – Indicates product demand and movement across stores.
- Top 10 Products by Revenue – Identifies high-performing products contributing the most to sales.
- Fast-Moving Products (by Quantity) – Highlights products with the highest sales volume.
- Store-Level Sales Performance – Compares revenue and quantity sold across different stores.
- SKU Contribution (%) – Shows each SKU’s percentage contribution to total sales.
- Average Sales per Product – Evaluates the average performance of individual products.
- Sales Trend Analysis – Tracks sales performance over time to identify growth or decline patterns.
